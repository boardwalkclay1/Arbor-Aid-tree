About Arbor Aid Tree Service

Owner — Victor Robinson

Victor Robinson is a 3rd‑generation lumberjack with over 20 years of professional tree service experience. Raised in a family of climbers, cutters, and sawyers, Victor learned the trade the same way his father and grandfather did — hands‑on, in the field, mastering every aspect of safe and precise tree work.

Victor specializes in:

  • ⚡ High‑risk tree removals near homes & power lines
  • 🌳 Precision trimming for long‑term tree health
  • 🪓 Stump grinding & root removal
  • 🌪 Emergency storm cleanup
  • 🏗 Lot clearing & land prep
  • 📐 Job planning, safety evaluation & crew coordination

Every job is treated like it’s his own property — clean, safe, and done right the first time. Victor personally oversees complex removals, storm damage work, and commercial projects to ensure the highest level of professionalism.
